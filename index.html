Space Pinball

A pinball table among the stars.
Play solo or take turns with a friend, peer-to-peer.

Tap the left or right side of the table to fire the flippers. Hold the launcher to charge the plunger, release to shoot. Clear drop targets, light S-P-A-C-E, and pop bumpers to rack up points. Three balls per turn — high score wins.
